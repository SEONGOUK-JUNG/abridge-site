About ABRIDGE

Bridge the World
with Trust & Trade

ABRIDGE is a global trading company integrating four business pillars: food security, green materials, chemical distribution, and strategic minerals.

Anchored in Korea and China, we operate a stable supply chain and quality-focused trading system, supported by official credentials including the aT (Korea Agro-Fisheries & Food Trade Corporation) State Trading bidding qualification and direct supply contracts with Chinese listed companies and specialized firms.

With trust and transparency, we facilitate cross-border resource cooperation and build the foundation for future industries — growing as a true global Bridge.
